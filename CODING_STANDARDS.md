# Coding standards

## Product contracts

For changes or reviews involving access, approvals, storage, memory, or background
execution, read the relevant sections of
[docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md](docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md), especially
Safety and privacy, Operational requirements, and Acceptance criteria. Those
sections own the product's isolation, persistence, and release requirements.

For setup and available commands, inspect [README.md](README.md) and
[package.json](package.json). Follow the installed configuration rather than
maintaining a second list of commands or compiler settings here.
